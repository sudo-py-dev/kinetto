# Sample artwork

These original vector illustrations were created for Kinetto. They are distributed under the repository's MIT OR Apache-2.0 license and may be reused in videos, including commercial projects. No online account or runtime download is required. Preserve the applicable license notices when redistributing the source assets.

- **Quiet landscape** — layered mountains and a lake at sunset.
- **Studio garden** — a warm interior and a sculptural plant.
- **Orbit study** — an abstract geometric still life.
- **Birthday cake** — a two-tier cake with candles, bunting and confetti.
- **Party balloons** — a bunch of balloons against an evening sky.
- **Seaside** — a beach umbrella, towel and ball at sunset.
- **City lights** — a night skyline with lit windows and a crescent moon.
- **Wildflowers** — a meadow of flowers with two butterflies.

The last five are drawn by `draw.py`, which writes each SVG and its PNG from the same list of shapes (run `python3 assets/samples/draw.py`; needs Pillow).

The SVG files are editable sources. Matching 1600 × 1200 PNGs are bundled in the app so templates, offline previews, saved projects and exports all use the same artwork. Template projects embed only the images they use when saved.
