# Placing music

Music belongs to the video timeline and is independent of slides.

- Choosing a soundtrack in a slide's Sound panel starts the song from its beginning at that slide's start. Choosing the same soundtrack again also restarts its placement there.
- In Timeline, grab the middle of the green music clip to move it to any time. Hold **Shift** to snap either end to a slide boundary.
- A full-video soundtrack can move later; it still stops at the video's end. A trimmed clip keeps its duration where it fits, and shortens at the video edge. Dragging back during the same gesture restores its original duration.
- Drag the left edge to trim the song's beginning; this also changes the source offset. Drag the right edge to change its stop time. Moving the whole clip preserves its source offset.
- Use **Start at this slide**, **Start at playhead**, or **From video start** under Sound for precise placement without dragging. The Music starts slider also works independently of slides.
- Enable **Until the video ends** to keep the soundtrack open-ended when the video grows. Turn it off to choose a stop time.

Placement supports save, undo and redo. Each completed drag is one undo step.
Moving or reordering slides does not move music that has already been placed.
