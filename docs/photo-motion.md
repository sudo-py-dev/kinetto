# Photo motion

Select a picture and open **Photo motion** in the inspector. Choose a movement,
set its amount, then use **Replay** in the Animation group to watch the slide.
Motion runs from the picture's appearance until the end of the slide. Longer
slides move more slowly. Entrance and exit animations still work independently.
Preview and video export use the same renderer. Changes support undo and save.

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Still | No continuous movement; keeps entrance and exit animations. |
| Zoom in | Slowly moves towards the centre of the photo. |
| Zoom out | Starts closer and gradually reveals the scene. |
| Drift left / right | Moves sideways with extra crop to keep the frame filled. |
| Breathe | One gentle zoom in and out over the slide. |

**Amount** controls extra magnification, from 2% to 40%. Movement eases at both
ends. The photo stays clipped to its frame and rounded corners; text and other
items remain in place. Keep important subjects near the centre for zooms.

Three one-click examples are included:

- **Travel drift**: 18% sideways movement, suited to landscape and travel imagery.
- **Elegant reveal**: 10% zoom out, suited to wedding and invitation imagery.
- **Product focus**: 16% zoom in, suited to interiors and product details.

Starter templates include subtle photo motion. Existing saved projects remain
still until a movement is selected.

These are original motion presets inspired by the supplied template-gallery
reference and Canva's public [travel](https://www.canva.com/video-editor/templates/travel/),
[wedding](https://www.canva.com/video-editor/templates/wedding/) and
[photo animation](https://www.canva.com/features/photo-animation/) pages.
They do not import a Canva project or its assets.
